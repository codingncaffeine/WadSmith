## What's New

- **Building in 3D** — Every tool now works in the 3D view, with handles for corners, walls and window sills, and a hint saying what the tool would do where the pointer is.
- **Your own sounds and music** — Drop a sound file on the map to place it there, or a music file to make it the map's music.
- **Atmosphere by name** — Give a room an atmosphere by name, such as cave, hell glow, toxic or moonlit.
- **Pictures straight onto the map** — Pictures dropped from the file manager or pasted go straight onto the map.
- **Decorations from pictures and GIFs** — Turn any picture or GIF into a decoration to place on the map; its Inspector sets how tall it stands and whether it blocks the way.
- **Signs** — Write words on a wall in the game's own letters or a computer font, and change them later from the Inspector.
- **Videos** — Put a video on a wall, door or floor (needs ffmpeg).
- **Mirrors and camera screens** — Make a wall a mirror that reflects its room, or a screen that shows what a camera elsewhere sees.
- **Edit a picture in another program** — Open one of your own pictures in your image editor and see the map change when you save it.
- **Moving floors on the plan** — Animated floors play on the 2D plan at the picture's own pace.
- **Share check** — Export now says what came from games other players must own.
- **Classic-port projects** — In a project for a classic port, what only GZDoom has is greyed with the reason, and the Objects shelf shows faint what the port cannot use.

## What's Fixed

- **Quake monsters in GZDoom** — Quake monsters placed from the Objects shelf now show their model in GZDoom.
- **Duke Nukem 3D enemies in GZDoom** — A map with Duke Nukem 3D's Lizard Trooper carried in no longer stops GZDoom from loading.
