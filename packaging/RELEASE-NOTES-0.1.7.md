## What's New

- **Packs made for another pack** — A fix or add-on made for a bigger pack, whose maps show that pack's textures, is recognised when the two lie in one folder or one ZIP: its card says what it is loaded over, and playing it or opening one of its maps loads that pack first.
- **Add WADs** — The Add games button at the top is now Add WADs: a folder added with it is searched for map packs as well as games.

## What's Fixed

- **A map pack taken for the game itself** — A big pack with its own palette, player and status bar could pass for a copy of Doom II and take the game's place: maps then played and opened over it, looking wrong and missing most sounds. A base game is now told from a pack by more than its pictures.
- **Packs missing from a game's page** — A pack that shows its own patches straight on its walls is listed under the game it is for, packs are matched again when a game's file changes, and a pack that uses only its own textures is no longer listed under games of another family.
- **The same WAD listed twice** — A WAD kept in its ZIP and also taken out of it is one card.
- **Walls drawn as missing** — Walls and floors that show a patch by its own name, as GZDoom allows, are drawn on the plan and in the 3D view, and Problems no longer calls their names unknown.
