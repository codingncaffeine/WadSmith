## What's New

- **Windows** — WadSmith now comes as a Windows zip too: unpack it anywhere and run wadsmith.exe.
- **Free monsters, weapons, items and props** — Choose Realm667 on the Objects shelf to browse over a thousand community-made objects with their pictures; drag one onto the map and it is downloaded and brought into your project with its credits.
- **Free texture packs** — Choose Texture packs on the Textures shelf for the Doom Wiki's picks (OTEX, cc4-tex, GothicDM and more) and Realm667's texture stock; click one to download it, then drag its textures onto walls and floors. Type a name to look for more on idgames.
- **Freedoom and Blasphemer from the shelves** — While you do not have them, both free games are offered on the shelves' chooser; one click fetches them and their textures and objects join the shelves.
- **Credits kept for you** — Everything downloaded keeps its author's credits and permissions in the project's credits folder, and they travel with an exported .pk3.
- **Export for the classic ports** — Ctrl+E exports a project as a .pk3 for GZDoom or a WAD for vanilla, limit-removing, Boom or MBF21 ports, with your own pictures carried in and a list of exactly what each map loses.
- **Play in a classic port** — The Play button's menu plays the map in Chocolate Doom, Crispy Doom or dsda-doom when they are installed.

## What's Fixed

- **Windows folders** — On Windows, WadSmith keeps its files, finds the engines and looks for your games where Windows keeps them.
- **Files held by another program** — Opening and saving a map no longer fails when a virus scanner or indexer briefly holds the file.
