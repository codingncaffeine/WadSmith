## What's New

- **Doors that slide or swing** — Any door can slide aside or swing on a hinge, as Hexen's do, alone or as two halves that part: choose it under Opens in the Inspector or with Ctrl+K (GZDoom).
- **Doors in every game** — Heretic, Hexen and Strife doors get their own pictures and keys, and Strife maps can have Strife's own sliding doors.
- **Polyobjects where the game puts them** — Sliding and swinging doors and Hexen's other polyobjects show in their places on the plan and in the 3D view, and say what they are.
- **Hubs** — Map settings can put a map in one of the game's hubs or a hub of your own, whose maps keep their state while the player keeps their keys (GZDoom).
- **Hexen map settings** — A Hexen map's name, music and sky can be changed, and it keeps its hub, skies and number.
- **Hexen's three heroes** — A Hexen object's Inspector shows and changes which of the Fighter, Cleric and Mage it is for.
- **Managing a project's maps** — The maps list adds, copies, moves and deletes a project's maps.
- **Raw fields** — Set any action, its arguments and any map field by hand from the Inspector.
- **Files WadSmith writes** — Read each file WadSmith writes for GZDoom in a coloured text view.
- **Your own keys** — Give any command a key of your own from the command palette.
- **Disconnect rooms** — Separate rooms that touch only at a corner.
- **Vanilla view limits** — Problems and Export say where a view would overflow vanilla Doom's limits.

## What's Fixed

- **Wide door pictures** — Choosing a wide door picture such as BIGDOOR2 now widens the doorway, so the whole picture shows.
- **Door looks and locks** — The Inspector's door pictures and locks now show in Heretic, Hexen and Strife, and in Doom with a named look.
