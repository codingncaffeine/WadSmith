## What's New

- **Every map for a game, as cards** — A game's page shows its maps as sets, each a card with a picture: the game's own maps, its official add-ons (Deathkings under Hexen) and every collection of map packs found in your folders, a ZIP of WADs or a loose WAD. A card's picture is the pack's own title screen, or its biggest map drawn as the automap shows it. Click a card for its maps, Back for the cards; the search box finds a set or a map.
- **Play a game as it is** — Right-click a game in the list of games, or a card, and choose Play: it starts in UZDoom or GZDoom from the title screen, with your own engine settings and saves, the pack or add-on loaded over the game.

## What's Fixed

- **The game's own maps** — They were offered only under Remix a map; they are now the first card on the game's page.
- **Clicking or deleting a window** — Any part of a window is the window, and deleting it closes the wall it stood in, instead of raising the floor onto the sill.
- **Clicking or deleting a switch** — A switch clicked in the 3D view shows the switch; deleting it closes its alcove and the wall takes the texture beside it.
- **Deleting the wall between a room and a step** — The step joins the room, instead of the room taking the step's height.
