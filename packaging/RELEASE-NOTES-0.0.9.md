## What's New

- **Made maps play more like the best community maps** — "Make me a map" now builds every map with what acclaimed community maps have in common: loops and one-way shortcuts back, windows into the next room, balconies with monsters above you, drops into lower rooms, and traps that spring when you pick up a key.
- **Hazards with a purpose** — Nukage, lava and water now run as channels along walls, rivers with a bridge where you cross, or flooded halls with dry walkways and an island, instead of small squares in the middle of a floor.
- **Light that comes from somewhere** — Made maps place their game's own lamps and torches in brighter patches under a light panel, with flickering torches and glowing hazards.
- **Power-ups where they help** — Made maps hand out a berserk early, a backpack about halfway, a megasphere in a secret, a radiation suit by the nukage and invulnerability before a boss fight, with tighter ammunition and health that the Challenge slider still sets.
- **More varied rooms** — Made maps change their look every few rooms, trim balconies, columns and window frames, and mix great halls, small chambers, bevelled corners and more sky.
- **Find and replace** — Ctrl+F finds pictures, objects, actions, room effects and tags across the map and replaces them in one step.
- **Slant** — The Inspector can slant a selection across or up by an angle.
- **Sound zones** — "Show sound zones" in the command palette colours the plan by where a shot is heard.
- **A switch with no switch picture** — Problems now points out a switch wall that shows no switch picture, so the player can see it.
