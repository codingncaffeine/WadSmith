## What's New

- **Monsters and objects from add-ons** — The monsters and objects an add-on's ZScript or DECORATE defines are on the Objects shelf by name and kind, and the plan and the 3D view draw them with their own pictures.
- **Pick walls and floors in the 3D view** — Click one and Shift+click others: a texture let go on any of them goes on just those, and Delete deletes just those.
- **Doors through walls** — The Door tool puts a door through a closed piece of wall, from the room on one side to the room on the other.

## What's Fixed

- **Clicking a door** — Any part of a door, its frame, its sides or its face in the 3D view, now shows the door's settings, including how it opens.
- **Deleting a door** — The door goes whole and the wall it stood in closes up like the wall beside it, instead of breaking the doorway.
- **Deleting the wall beside a door** — No longer turns the room solid.
