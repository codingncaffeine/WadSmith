## What's New

- **Arrow keys on the plan** — With something selected, the arrow keys move it a grid step (Shift: one unit); with nothing selected they scroll the plan. With the Doom Builder keys the arrows scroll and Ctrl with them moves the selection.
- **Lengths as you draw** — Each side of a room drawn corner by corner shows its length, the side to the pointer its angle too, and dragging shows the length of every wall it stretches.
- **Box select with Ctrl** — A box drawn with Ctrl takes away from the selection, and with Ctrl+Shift keeps only what is in both.
- **Go to a point** — The status bar shows where the pointer is on the plan; click it, or press Ctrl+G, to bring any point to the middle of the plan.
- **Textures this map uses** — The Textures shelf has a "Used in this map" group.
- **More link lines** — Objects with an action of their own, actions that work on objects by their id, and patrol and camera paths show their dashed links on the plan.
- **Undo list** — The Versions panel lists this session's steps; click one to go back, or forward, to it.
- **Add-ons** — Load your own WADs, PK3s and folders with a project from the Inspector: their textures go on the Textures shelf and Play loads them too.
