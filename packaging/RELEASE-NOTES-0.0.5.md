## What's New

- **Remix or start fresh** — Pick any map of the games you own and make it yours, or start a new map; your game files are never changed. No game? WadSmith fetches Freedoom for you.
- **Plan and 3D side by side** — Draw rooms of any shape on the plan and see them at once in a 3D view matched to GZDoom's own picture; Tab swaps to the 3D view alone.
- **Smart building** — Doors, lifts, stairs, windows, switches, secrets, keys, teleporters, exits, lights, platforms and water, each made with a click and recognised in any existing map.
- **Edit in 3D** — Drop textures and objects onto what you see, and move floors, ceilings and textures right in the 3D view.
- **Play in one key** — F9 plays the map in GZDoom after a check that offers to fix what would break the game; Shift+F9 starts you where the 3D view stands.
- **Problems, progression and balance** — A live Problems panel, the way from start to exit step by step, and a meter that says whether the ammunition is enough for the monsters on each difficulty.
- **Learn as you go** — A guided first map, hands-on Guide chapters, "What is this?" for anything you point at, examples from the games' own maps, a feature tour, and presets such as Low, Normal, Tall and Huge in place of numbers.
- **For veterans too** — Classic names, Doom Builder keys, a command palette (Ctrl+K), and tooltips full, brief or off.
- **Never lose work** — Every change is kept as you go, with versions to go back to.
- **A command-line tool** — `wadsmith-cli`, installed beside the app, turns a game into folders of pictures, sounds, music and maps, starts a remix, checks a file and more; `wadsmith-cli help` lists it all.
