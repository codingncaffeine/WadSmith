## What's New

- **Fly by default** — The 3D view opens flying, at the player start and facing the way the player faces.
- **Open a friend's map** — Open a map file… on the start screen opens a WAD someone gave you, and a folder of maps offers each one; a strip under the top bar says when something could not be opened.
- **New WADs show up by themselves** — Games and maps put in your game folders are listed straight away.

## What's Fixed

- **No holes of sky** — Columns, doors, windows and switch alcoves out under the open sky have a ceiling of their own, so the game no longer shows a column or slot of sky where they stand.
- **Doors that fit their pictures** — Made maps put each door in a doorway as tall as its picture, so the picture no longer repeats up the door; secret doors stay flush with the wall.
- **Other games' monsters, whole** — In made maps, Strife's Stalker and Heretic's Iron Lich bring their pictures instead of an error sign, and Hexen's monsters bring their sounds.
- **Closet monsters get out** — Monster closets hold only monsters that fit through their doorway.
